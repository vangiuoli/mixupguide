# Verification method

MixupGuide separates objective mechanics from strategy. `verified-documentation` means a current character or mechanics page explicitly supports the property. It does **not** certify reaction, evasive, or punish timing. Those claims require repeated live tests.

## Evidence priority

1. Current in-game or official update log
2. Repeated private-server live tests
3. Current Jujutsu Shenanigans Wiki character/mechanics page
4. Recent video showing the exact interaction
5. Clearly labeled community strategy

The linked wiki archive adapts CC BY-SA 4.0 material. This project independently summarizes factual mechanics, links the supporting section, and does not copy extended prose. MixupGuide is an unofficial fan guide and is not endorsed by JJS, Roblox, or the wiki.

## Live-test checklist

For each variant record patch, date, tester, platform, approximate ping, repetitions, and a clip/reference. Test front/side/rear block, perfect block and chip; early/on-cue/late side dash, backdash, jump and wall behavior; ragdoll and evasive timing; each applicable counter class; and point-blank/max-range whiff, blocked, variant-failure and dash-available punish states. At least five repeatable trials are required before `verified-live`.

This environment cannot enter Roblox. All reaction, punish, and evasive guarantees therefore remain `needs-live-test` in the product even when a documented property suggests a likely answer.
